> Hey! Starting this Wednesday my daughter has soccer after school, and I have to pick her up at 5:30 every Wednesday through the fall. That's right when I usually do my easy run. Can you rework my plan around it?

## What the message leaves out (on purpose)
- Whether Annie is willing to run on Wednesday mornings
- Whether someone else can watch the kids if she runs right after pickup
- Whether she wants the change for this week only, or for every remaining Wednesday (the calendar says the pickup repeats through Dec 9)

A good agent notices these gaps and asks before finalizing, one question at a time.
